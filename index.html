How to swap tokens on Solana

A same-chain swap exchanges one asset for another in your Solana wallet. Both the input and the received token stay on Solana.

  1. ⇄

    Choose a token pair

    Select the asset you hold and the asset you want. Use SOL for the network's native token or choose a USDC and USDT stablecoin pair.

  2. ≋

    Review the swap quote

    Open the app and enter your amount. Compare net output, price impact, minimum received and the full fee breakdown for the route.

  3. ✓

    Approve in your wallet

    Check the tokens and transaction details, then sign. Follow the confirmation status and check the received token balance in your wallet.

What 0.5% slippage means

Slippage tolerance sets how far execution can move against a quoted output. It is a price limit, not an extra fee.

How do I swap Solana tokens?

Choose the token you pay with and the token you want to receive, then open the swap app. Connect a Solana wallet, enter an amount, review the output and fees, and approve the transaction in your wallet. Keep SOL available for network fees.

What is the difference between SOL and Solana tokens?

Solana is the blockchain. SOL is its native asset, used to pay transaction fees and secure the network through staking. USDC and USDT on Solana are tokens issued on that same network. A SOL-to-USDC swap changes the asset while keeping the funds on Solana.

How much does a Solana swap cost?

The network base fee is 0.000005 SOL per signature, plus any priority fee. Liquidity pools charge trading fees, and a route may include a platform fee. Creating a token account also requires a rent deposit. Review the full cost and net output before approval.

How long does a Solana token swap take?

A swap moves from submission to processing, confirmation, and finalization. Network congestion, the priority fee, and transaction complexity affect the time. Check the transaction status before submitting the same exchange again.

What slippage should I use for a Solana swap?

Set slippage according to the pair, amount, and liquidity. A lower tolerance gives a tighter minimum output but can cause a transaction to fail if prices move. A higher tolerance accepts a wider change in execution price. Review minimum received separately from price impact.

Can I swap USDC for USDT on Solana?

Yes. Select USDC as the input and USDT as the output, with both tokens on Solana. Compare a direct pool with a route through SOL. Stablecoin prices and pool fees affect the amount received, so compare the final output rather than assuming a 1:1 conversion.

Can I swap ETH on Solana?

ETH on Solana is a token representation with a specific mint and bridge origin. Match both before trading it. Native ETH on Ethereum first requires a cross-chain transfer into a supported Solana token; a same-chain swap exchanges assets already on Solana.

Why did my Solana swap fail?

Common causes include insufficient SOL for fees, an expired transaction, a price move beyond the slippage limit, or a change in pool liquidity. Check the wallet balance and transaction status, refresh the quote, and review the route before retrying. A processed failed transaction can still incur a network fee.
